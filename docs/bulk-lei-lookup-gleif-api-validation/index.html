How to validate LEI codes and find the LEI for a list of companies in bulk

Updated 2026-10-28 · Tool used: LEI Lookup - Bulk GLEIF LEI Search, Validation & Parents on Apify · Price: $1 per 1,000 LEI records found; invalid check digits and no-match rows are free

Compliance, onboarding and regulatory reporting teams often need the LEI of hundreds of counterparties, or need to check that the LEIs in a CRM are still valid and renewed. The GLEIF website search is built for one entity at a time.

This guide shows how to check a whole list in one run against the official GLEIF API (open CC0 data): verify LEI check digits, look up legal name, legal and head office address, register number, entity status and renewal date, find the LEI from a company name, BIC or ISIN, and add the direct and ultimate parent company.

Open LEI Lookup on Apify →

Steps

  1. Add LEIs to leis, company names to companyNames (optionally narrowed by country), or BICs and ISINs to bics and isins.
  2. Keep includeParents on to get the direct and ultimate parent, or the reason reported when there is none.
  3. Export as CSV and filter on registrationStatus, renewalOverdue and matchScore to spot lapsed LEIs and weak name matches.

Python

    "HWUPKR0MPOU8FGXBT394",

for item in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():

FAQ

How do I check if an LEI is valid?

The actor first verifies the ISO 17442 check digits, so typos are flagged without a request and cost nothing. Valid codes are then looked up at GLEIF; registrationStatus and renewalOverdue show lapsed LEIs.

Can I find the LEI from a company name?

Yes. Name search matches legal-form suffixes both ways (AG and Aktiengesellschaft, Ltd and Limited), prefers the head office over branches and returns a matchScore, where 1 means an exact legal name match.

Is GLEIF data free to use?

Yes. GLEIF publishes LEI data as open data under CC0, so you can use the results commercially.

Can I map a BIC or ISIN to an LEI?

Yes, through GLEIF's official BIC-to-LEI and ISIN-to-LEI mappings.
